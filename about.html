About me

Curious about
what’s inside.

I’m Maharab Mohammed. I’ve always loved how computers look and how they work. What fascinates me most is that so many individual parts work together at the same time to make a computer function.

That curiosity makes me want to understand what each part does and how everything fits together. It goes beyond computers, too. I’m also interested in how phones, smartwatches, and smart home appliances work.

My education

Turning curiosity into learning.

I’m majoring in Electrical and Computer Engineering Technology at New Jersey Institute of Technology.

I want to build strong technical skills through my class labs and join a hands-on engineering club.

Looking ahead

From understanding devices
to helping create them.

Eventually, I’d love to work for a tech company helping design and test power or smart devices. Learning how individual components work together is what draws me toward that goal.

Outside of my studies

Technology, soccer, and Minecraft.

Technology is a big interest of mine, but I also enjoy soccer and Minecraft.
