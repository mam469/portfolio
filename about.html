About me

Curious about
what’s inside.

I’m Maharab Mohammed. I’ve always loved how computers look and how they work. What fascinates me most is that so many individual parts work together at the same time to make a computer function.

That curiosity makes me want to understand what each part does and how everything fits together. It goes beyond computers, too. I’m also interested in how phones, smartwatches, and smart home appliances work.
